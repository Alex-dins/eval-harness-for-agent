# Nimbus Refund Policy

Nimbus offers a 30-day money-back guarantee on all paid plans. If you are not
satisfied, contact support within 30 days of your payment for a full refund.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method and take 5 to 10 business days
to appear on your statement.

Partial refunds are available for annual plans cancelled after 30 days: the unused
whole months are refunded, minus a 10% administrative fee. Monthly plans are not
eligible for partial refunds after the 30-day window.

To request a refund, email billing@nimbus.example with your account email and
invoice number.
