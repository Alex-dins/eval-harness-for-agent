# Nimbus Security & Compliance

Nimbus encrypts all data in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher, and encrypts data at
rest using AES-256.

Nimbus is SOC 2 Type II compliant and undergoes an annual third-party audit. A copy
of the latest SOC 2 report is available to Enterprise customers under NDA.

Single Sign-On (SSO) via SAML 2.0 is available on the Enterprise plan. Supported
identity providers include Okta, Azure AD, and Google Workspace.

Data is hosted in AWS regions in the United States (us-east-1) and the European
Union (eu-west-1). Customers may choose their data residency region during
onboarding on the Enterprise plan.
