# Nimbus Pricing Plans

Nimbus offers three subscription tiers, billed monthly or annually. Annual billing
saves 20% compared to monthly.

- **Starter** — $12 per user / month. Includes up to 5 projects and 10 GB of storage.
- **Team** — $29 per user / month. Includes unlimited projects, 100 GB of storage,
  and priority email support.
- **Enterprise** — custom pricing. Includes SSO, audit logs, a dedicated success
  manager, and a 99.9% uptime SLA.

All paid plans include a 14-day free trial. No credit card is required to start the
trial.
