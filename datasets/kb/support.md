# Nimbus Support Hours

Nimbus support is available Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM US Eastern
Time (ET). We are closed on weekends and US public holidays.

- **Starter** plan: email support with a 48-hour response target.
- **Team** plan: priority email support with a 24-hour response target.
- **Enterprise** plan: 24/7 support via email and phone, with a 1-hour response
  target for critical issues.

You can reach support at support@nimbus.example. For urgent Enterprise issues,
use the dedicated phone line listed in your onboarding email.
