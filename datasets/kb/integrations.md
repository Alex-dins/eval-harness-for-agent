# Nimbus Integrations

Nimbus integrates with popular tools to fit your workflow:

- **Slack** — get project notifications in any channel.
- **GitHub** — link commits and pull requests to Nimbus tasks.
- **Google Calendar** — sync task due dates to your calendar.
- **Zapier** — connect Nimbus to 5,000+ apps.

Integrations are configured under **Settings → Integrations**. Team and Enterprise
plans can connect an unlimited number of integrations; the Starter plan is limited
to two.
