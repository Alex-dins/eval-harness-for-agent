# Exporting Your Data from Nimbus

You can export your data at any time from **Settings → Data → Export**. Exports are
generated as a ZIP archive containing JSON and CSV files.

- Project data, tasks, and comments are exported as JSON.
- Time-tracking and billing records are exported as CSV.
- File attachments are included in an `attachments/` folder inside the ZIP.

Large exports are processed in the background. When the export is ready, Nimbus
emails a secure download link to the account owner. The link expires after 72 hours.

There is no limit on how often you can export your data, and exports are available
on all plans, including Starter.
